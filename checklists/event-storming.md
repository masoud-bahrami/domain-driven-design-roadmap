### Event Storming Checklist

- [ ] Gather domain experts, developers, testers, and other relevant participants.
- [ ] Prepare the physical or digital space.
- [ ] Define the scope and purpose of the session.
- [ ] Identify business-relevant Domain Events and place them on a timeline.
- [ ] Identify Commands that trigger events.
- [ ] Identify potential Aggregates or other decision points where useful.
- [ ] Identify external systems and actors.
- [ ] Identify read models and policies where relevant.
- [ ] Explore exceptions, conflicts, alternative flows, and hotspots.
- [ ] Record insights, assumptions, disagreements, and open questions.
- [ ] Validate the results with participants, especially domain experts.

**Note:** Event Storming is a collaborative exploration technique, not a diagramming exercise or a process for mechanically deriving a domain model. The value lies in the conversations, discoveries, disagreements, and questions that emerge while exploring how the business works. Do not force every concept into a predefined notation or treat potential Aggregates and Bounded Contexts as final design decisions. Let the understanding emerge from the discussion.

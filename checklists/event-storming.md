# Event Storming Checklist

- [ ] Gather domain experts, developers, testers, and other relevant participants.
- [ ] Prepare the physical or digital space.
- [ ] Define the scope of the session.
- [ ] Identify business-relevant Domain Events and place them on a timeline.
- [ ] Identify Commands that trigger events.
- [ ] Identify Aggregates or other decision points where useful.
- [ ] Identify external systems and actors.
- [ ] Identify read models and policies where relevant.
- [ ] Explore exceptions, conflicts, and hotspots.
- [ ] Record insights, assumptions, and open questions.
- [ ] Validate the result with participants.

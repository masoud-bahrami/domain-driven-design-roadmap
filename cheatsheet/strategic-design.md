# Strategic Design

1. Identify the business capabilities and subdomains.
2. Classify subdomains as Core, Supporting, or Generic based on business strategy.
3. Discover where language, rules, and models change meaning.
4. Define Bounded Contexts around coherent models and responsibilities.
5. Map relationships, ownership, and integration patterns.
6. Revisit boundaries when the business or the model changes.

A Context Map is a communication and design artifact, not just a system diagram.

# Books

## Domain-Driven Design

- Eric Evans, *Domain-Driven Design: Tackling Complexity in the Heart of Software*. The foundational reference often called the Blue Book.
- Vaughn Vernon, *Implementing Domain-Driven Design*. A detailed implementation-oriented companion.
- Vaughn Vernon, *Domain-Driven Design Distilled*. A concise introduction to strategic and tactical DDD.
- Vlad Khononov, *Learning Domain-Driven Design*. A modern, accessible learning path.
- Stefan Hofer and Henning Schwentner, *Domain Storytelling: A Collaborative, Visual, and Agile Way to Build Domain-Driven Software*.
- Alberto Brandolini, *Introducing EventStorming*. A resource for collaborative domain exploration.

## Design, programming, and testing

- Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson, and John Vlissides, *Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software*.
- Brett McLaughlin, Gary Pollice, and David West, *Head First Object-Oriented Analysis and Design*.
- Robert C. Martin, *Clean Code*.
- Martin Fowler, *Refactoring: Improving the Design of Existing Code*, with Kent Beck.
- Michael Feathers, *Working Effectively with Legacy Code*.
- Kent Beck, *Test-Driven Development: By Example*.
- Steve Freeman and Nat Pryce, *Growing Object-Oriented Software, Guided by Tests*.
- Gojko Adzic, *Specification by Example: How Successful Teams Deliver the Right Software*.
- Scott Wlaschin, *Domain Modeling Made Functional*.
- Kevlin Henney (editor), *97 Things Every Programmer Should Know*.

The original roadmap also mentions language-specific references such as *C# in Depth* and *Effective Java*.

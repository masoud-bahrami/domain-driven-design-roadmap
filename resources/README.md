# Resources

Learning Domain-Driven Design requires more than reading about patterns and concepts. Books provide depth, articles explore specific ideas, talks expose you to practitioners' experiences, and communities offer opportunities to discuss and challenge your understanding.

This section collects resources that complement the learning roadmap. It includes foundational DDD material, software design and architecture references, practical techniques, and communities where you can continue learning.

## Resource Collections

### [Books](books.md)

A curated selection of books covering:
- Domain-Driven Design fundamentals and implementation.
- Strategic and tactical modeling.
- Software design, architecture, and refactoring.
- Testing, collaborative modeling, and functional programming.

### [Articles and Papers](articles.md)

Selected articles, essays, and papers that explore DDD concepts, software design, architecture, and related practices in greater depth.

### [Talks and Videos](talks-and-videos.md)

Presentations, conference recordings, and video collections from experienced practitioners.

Useful for discovering different perspectives, seeing modeling techniques in practice, and learning from real-world experiences.

### [Tools for DDD Work](tools.md)

Tools that support collaborative domain exploration, diagramming, documentation, communication, and implementation.

These are optional aids, not prerequisites for applying DDD.

### [Communities](communities.md)

Communities, conferences, online groups, and resource collections where you can exchange experiences, ask questions, discover new material, and participate in the wider DDD ecosystem.

## How to Use These Resources

You do not need to consume every resource or follow a single reading order.

Choose resources according to what you are trying to understand:

- **New to DDD?** Start with a foundational book and introductory talks before exploring the more specialized material.
- **Exploring a complex domain?** Look for practical material on EventStorming, Domain Storytelling, Example Mapping, and collaborative modeling.
- **Designing Bounded Contexts?** Study strategic design, Subdomains, Context Mapping, and integration patterns.
- **Improving your domain model?** Explore Aggregates, Value Objects, Domain Events, Supple Design, and refactoring.
- **Making architectural decisions?** Read about modular monoliths, microservices, Hexagonal Architecture, Clean Architecture, CQRS, and Event Sourcing.
- **Looking for practical experience?** Join communities, attend talks, and discuss real modeling and design challenges with other practitioners.

## Contributing Resources

This collection is intended to evolve.

When suggesting a resource, include its title, author or speaker, publication or event, URL, and a short explanation of its learning value.

Prefer resources that:
- Explain the reasoning behind design decisions, not just the mechanics of patterns.
- Connect concepts to realistic business problems.
- Share practical experiences, including mistakes and trade-offs.
- Help readers develop a deeper understanding of modeling and software design.
- Remain relevant to the principles and practices of DDD.

Where a recommendation cannot be verified, mark it as a research gap rather than inventing a title, author, or link.

**The objective is not to collect the largest library of DDD material. It is to build the understanding needed to recognize problems, ask better questions, and make better design decisions.**

# Talks and Videos

The original roadmap recommends introductory DDD videos and online Event Storming tutorials, but does not name specific videos or channels. Rather than inventing references, use these starting points to find current material:

- EventStorming: https://www.eventstorming.com/
- DDD Crew: https://github.com/ddd-crew

Contributions of specific talks should include the speaker, title, event or channel, URL, and a short note explaining who will benefit.

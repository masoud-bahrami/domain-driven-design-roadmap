# Talks and Videos

Talks and videos offer a different way to learn Domain-Driven Design. They expose you to the reasoning, experiences, and disagreements of practitioners who have applied DDD in real projects.

This collection includes introductory talks, collaborative modeling demonstrations, and more specialized presentations. Each recommendation includes its speaker, event or source, and the audience likely to benefit.

## Introduction to Domain-Driven Design

### [What is DDD?](https://github.com/ddd-crew/free-ddd-learning-resources)

- **Speaker:** Eric Evans
- **Event:** DDD Europe 2019
- **Level:** Beginner
- **Focus:** An introduction to Domain-Driven Design from its originator.

A useful starting point for understanding the purpose of DDD and the relationship between domain knowledge, models, and software design.

**Who should watch:** Developers, architects, business analysts, product people, and anyone encountering DDD for the first time.

### [Introduction to Domain-Driven Design and Collaborative Software Design](https://virtualddd.com/sessions/introduction-to-domain-driven-design-and-collaborative-software-design-kenny-baas-schwegler/)

- **Speaker:** Kenny Baas-Schwegler
- **Event:** Virtual DDD, September 2024
- **Level:** Beginner
- **Focus:** DDD fundamentals, strategic and tactical design, and collaborative software design.

Explains why focusing exclusively on tactical patterns or strategic modeling can lead to problems, and why both perspectives matter.

**Who should watch:** Beginners and practitioners who want to understand DDD as a collaborative design discipline rather than a collection of coding patterns.

## EventStorming and Collaborative Modeling

### [50,000 Orange Stickies Later](https://www.eventstorming.com/resources/)

- **Speaker:** Alberto Brandolini
- **Event:** Explore DDD 2017
- **Level:** Beginner to Intermediate
- **Focus:** EventStorming formats, purposes, and practical applications.

One of the recommended starting points for understanding EventStorming beyond the mechanics of putting orange sticky notes on a wall.

It explores the different ways EventStorming can help people understand complex domains and collaborate around business processes.

**Who should watch:** Developers, architects, facilitators, business analysts, and people preparing to run EventStorming workshops.

### [100,000 Orange Stickies Later](https://www.eventstorming.com/resources/)

- **Speaker:** Alberto Brandolini
- **Event:** Øredev 2019
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Focus:** Experience and lessons from applying EventStorming at scale.

A useful follow-up for people who already understand the basic technique and want to explore its practical use in larger and more complex environments.

**Who should watch:** Experienced facilitators and teams applying collaborative modeling across organizational boundaries.

### [EventStorming for Fun and Profit](https://www.eventstorming.com/resources/)

- **Speaker:** Dan North
- **Event:** DDD eXchange London 2017
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Focus:** Applying EventStorming to collaborative discovery and software design.

An alternative perspective on the value of EventStorming and how collaborative exploration can help teams make better design decisions.

**Who should watch:** Developers, technical leads, and facilitators interested in improving collaborative modeling.

## Strategic Design and Architecture

### [Bounded Contexts](https://github.com/ddd-crew/free-ddd-learning-resources)

- **Speaker:** Cyrille Martraire
- **Source:** DDD Crew's free learning resources
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Focus:** Understanding and identifying Bounded Contexts.

A focused resource for exploring the role of semantic boundaries in DDD and how different models can coexist within the same organization.

**Who should watch:** Architects, technical leads, and developers working on large or evolving systems.

### [Emergent Boundaries](https://github.com/ddd-crew/free-ddd-learning-resources)

- **Speaker:** Matthias Verraes
- **Source:** DDD Crew's free learning resources
- **Level:** Intermediate to Advanced
- **Focus:** Discovering boundaries through domain exploration rather than imposing them prematurely.

Useful for understanding why Bounded Contexts should emerge from differences in language, knowledge, responsibility, and business behavior.

**Who should watch:** Experienced DDD practitioners and architects dealing with unclear or evolving domain boundaries.

### [Extreme Modelling Patterns](https://www.eventstorming.com/resources/)

- **Speaker:** Alberto Brandolini
- **Event:** DDD Europe 2023
- **Level:** Intermediate to Advanced
- **Focus:** Modeling patterns and collaborative exploration.

A more specialized presentation for practitioners interested in advanced modeling techniques and the evolution of collaborative design practices.

**Who should watch:** Experienced modelers, architects, and EventStorming facilitators.

## CQRS and Event Sourcing

- **Event:** NDC Oslo 2023

## Contributing Talks and Videos

This collection should grow through contributions from practitioners.

When recommending a talk or video, include:

- **Speaker:** Who presented it?
- **Title:** What is the exact title?
- **Event or channel:** Where was it presented or published?
- **URL:** Where can people watch it?
- **Level:** Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
- **Focus:** What does the presentation explore?
- **Audience:** Who will benefit most from watching it?

Prefer talks that share practical experiences, explain the reasoning behind design decisions, demonstrate techniques, or challenge established assumptions.

Avoid adding videos merely because they mention DDD in their title. A useful recommendation should provide meaningful learning value.

**The best talks do more than explain patterns. They show how practitioners think, make decisions, discover problems, and change their models when their understanding of the domain improves.**

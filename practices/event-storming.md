# Event Storming

Event Storming is a collaborative workshop format for exploring a business domain through events and the decisions, commands, actors, policies, and systems around them. The original roadmap identifies Big Picture, Process-Level, and Software Design formats.

## A practical session

1. Agree on the scope and invite people with relevant domain knowledge.
2. Identify domain events and place them in a plausible timeline.
3. Explore commands, actors, external systems, policies, and read models.
4. Discuss exceptions, hotspots, contradictions, and unanswered questions.
5. Identify possible boundaries and follow-up investigations.
6. Capture decisions and validate the result with participants.

Do not treat the sticky-note layout as the final domain model. It is a tool for discovery and conversation.
